Meet the author

I'm Missy. I've run the miles I'm asking you to run.

Running became my anchor at eleven years old — not because I was fast, but because after a trauma left me drowning in anxiety, it was the one thing that let me feel my pain instead of just carrying it.

Mile after mile, it became my teacher. Since that first 26.2-mile race, I've faced trials I didn't think I'd survive. Every single time, running taught me something I needed to know about endurance, resilience, and what it means to keep going.

That's why I wrote Unbreakable Sole — so you never have to run your hardest miles alone.
