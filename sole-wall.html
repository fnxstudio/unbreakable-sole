"There is no greater agony than bearing an untold story inside you."

Hello friends!

This is the SOLE Wall.

My purpose for writing UNBREAKABLE SOLE was to help others find their own story in the pages of my book.

This is where you get to share yours.

Read about others, and you'll quickly see what we all have in common:
We all have challenges... and we're all still here to talk about them.

As you read stories of what people have overcome, you'll notice a theme.

Strength. Courage. And heart.

This is a place of connection.
My hope is that as you read through these stories, you'll find someone who's fighting a battle similar to yours... or someone you can relate to... or maybe you'll gain a new perspective on your own struggles and find gratitude in the process.

Each person on this wall has shared a way to contact them if you'd like a new friend on the journey.

If you'd like to be added to the SOLE Wall, we would love to have you.
Please click the link to fill out an application.

Your story matters. And someone out there needs to hear it.
