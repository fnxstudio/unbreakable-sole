Don’t just read Unbreakable Sole… live it.

The SOLE-A-THON is a 6-week guided journey where we read five chapters (miles) each week and conquer a new leg of the course, each with its own powerful theme.

Step by step, you’ll break through mental and emotional barriers until you cross the finish line with a mindset that is truly unbreakable.

NOTIFY ME FIRST

When you join, you’ll get:

  • Digital PDF of the UNBREAKABLE SOLE book
  • Full Audiobook (so you can listen on the go)
  • Digital SOLE Journal with guided reflections
  • Weekly themed challenges that align with that week’s chapters
  • Weekly live meetups with Missy Wright (the author!) and fellow SOLE-A-THON participants
  • Daily movement challenge. Walk, run, or move in a way that works for you
  • Finisher’s Medal mailed to you at the end, celebrating your transformation
  • 90 Day access to the exclusive Hardy Club. A supportive community that encourages, challenges, and celebrates you every step of the way

This is a richly packed course that will leave you with more resilience, breakthroughs, and help you write the next chapter of your story, with a community that has your back.
