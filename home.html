10 Things This Journey Will Help You Discover

1. That you can keep going, even when everything in you feels broken
Some miles nearly undo us. These stories remind you you’re not alone.
2. Hope — for the big things and the little things
Keep your eyes on the prize ahead, and discover the “silver balloons” that will get you there.
3. How the impossible becomes possible when you break it down
The big things are more doable when we focus on one mile at a time.
4. The courage inside you that whispers, “Don’t you quit”
Fear doesn’t get the final say. Your heart does.
5. The strength you didn’t know you had
The kind that rises only in the most difficult places.
6. A new way to see your circumstances — with gratitude
Perspective that steadies you when your world gets turned upside down.
7. A love for your amazing, imperfect, one-of-a-kind self
Not because you’ve earned it… but because it’s already yours, and there’s only one YOU.
8. How to navigate hard personalities with grace, grit, and boundaries
Without losing yourself in the process.
9. The people meant to run beside you
Your pace runners, the ones who determine your destination, and the speed at which you get there.
10. And ultimately, the unbreakable soul inside you
The one that has survived every mile so far.
