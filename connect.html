The Story Too Raw for the Book

There was a story I couldn’t put in UNBREAKABLE SOLE.
Not because it didn’t fit…
But because it was the hardest experience of my entire life.
(Yep... even harder than losing Aaron, my grandparents, and Elliott)

The weekend that broke me open.
The mile I didn’t think I’d survive.
The one I couldn’t wrap my brain around for a long time.
Too personal, too emotional, too vulnerable to publish.

But it’s the story that shaped me the most… that taught me there is nothing I can’t survive after going through this experience.

And I want to share it with the readers who help this message reach more people.

When you leave a review and send me the link, you’ll receive:

An intimate look into the worst weekend of my life… and how I found a way through it.

Book Missy as a guest  on your podcast

If you host a podcast and want a guest who brings real stories, real resilience, and real heart — I’d love to join you.

I’ve been personally trained and mentored by world-renowned speaker Darren Hardy, and I bring a unique level of depth, storytelling, and audience engagement to every interview. I promise to leave your listeners captivated, encouraged, and thinking about their own stories in a whole new way.

I speak openly about anxiety, courage, childhood trauma, running, family curveballs, and the mile-by-mile journey behind UNBREAKABLE SOLE. My goal is to give your audience hope, tools they can use today, and a conversation they’ll remember.

Common topics include:

  • Turning your hardest story into strength

  • Mental toughness for real humans
